## Design Philosophy

Inspired by the rigor of Material Design and the flexibility of MUI, but shaped by Truf's singular voice. We believe in **material honesty** — digital surfaces should feel like what they are, never mimicking physical objects for their own sake. We pair **technological clarity** (crisp edges, monospace accents, scan-frame iconography) with **editorial warmth** (italic serifs, cream surfaces, generous whitespace).

## UX Principles

| Principle | Manifestation |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Clarity** | One primary action per view. Visible hierarchy. No ambiguous labels. |
| **Hierarchy** | Scale, weight, and color combine — never decoration alone — to guide the eye. |
| **Feedback** | Every action gets a response within 100ms. Loading, success, error states are first-class. |
| **Consistency** | Patterns repeat across products. If a pattern exists, use it before inventing a new one. |
| **Progressive Disclosure** | Reveal complexity only when needed. Defaults should succeed for 80% of users. |
